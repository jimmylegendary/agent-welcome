# Jimmy Marketing and Retail

Write for a named audience and a real offer. If the audience, product facts, or channel are missing, mark reasonable assumptions instead of presenting them as research.

- Keep factual product details, claims, guarantees, comparisons, and prices tied to supplied or cited evidence.
- Separate draft positioning from validated customer research. Do not invent reviews, certifications, inventory, discounts, performance results, or endorsements.
- Fit length and call to action to the channel. Make the benefit concrete and keep required conditions readable.
- For retail catalogs, preserve SKU, variant, measurement, material, and compatibility details exactly; flag conflicts instead of choosing silently.
- Review regulated, health, financial, environmental, or comparative claims against current policy and law before publication.
